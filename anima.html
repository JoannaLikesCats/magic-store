About the potion

Buff duration: flexible, Nerf duration: it varies on animals

Dream Petal, Spirit Bark, Moonstone Essence, Whiskas, Enchanted Feather

You can teleport your soul into an animal body

When you teleport back to your human body, you will act like the respective animal for a while. For example, if you teleported into a cat body, you will keep meowing for a while as a human and you will scratch your home furniture
